# Saw x
**Genre:** Horror, Thriller, Mystery
**Director:** Kevin Greuturt
**Release:** 2003

---

###Plot Summary
*Saw X* takes place between the events of *Saw I* and *Saw II*.  
John Kramer, also known as **Jigsaw**, travels to Mexico in search of a cure for his cancer.  
When he discovers the operation is a scam, he decides to teach the scammers a deadly lesson.

---

### Why I Like It
1. It shows a more emotional side of **John Kramer**.  
2. The traps are creative and intense.  
3. It mixes horror with moral lessons.

---

> “The rules of my games are simple. You either follow them or you don’t.” — Jigsaw  

---

- It’s one of the best-reviewed *Saw* films.  
- Fans loved its return to the original tone.  
- The story connects perfectly to the early movies.
